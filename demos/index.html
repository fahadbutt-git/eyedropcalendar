Eye Drop Tracker and its Oscar extension

Screen recordings of a real browser, clicking through the extension's setup and a visit. Eye Drop Tracker turns an eye drop regimen into a printed calendar, a prescription and phone reminders. The Oscar extension reads the drops from the chart and writes the finished plan back.

Every patient in these recordings is made up, and the Oscar shown is a stand-in that runs on a laptop. Clinicians check every drop against the chart before anything prints.
