# Confidentiality undertaking

For everyone who works on Eye Drop Tracker, including its makers, contributors with release access and anyone helping with testing or support. The clinic terms promise that each such person agrees to this in writing, as O. Reg. 329/04, section 6(1) asks of those who provide electronic services to health information custodians.

I agree that:

1. I will not seek, collect, use or disclose personal health information from any clinic, patient or chart in the course of work on Eye Drop Tracker.
2. If I see personal health information by accident, for example in an email, screenshot or support request, I will not use or share it, I will tell the privacy officer the same day, and I will delete it as soon as the privacy officer confirms.
3. I will develop and test only with made-up patients, on demo or stand-in systems. I will never connect development work to a live clinic chart.
4. I will not add code that sends information from the browser, extension or app to any server other than the clinic's own systems, without the written approval of both makers and an update to the privacy notice and security page.
5. I will keep my development accounts, signing keys and store accounts secured with multi-factor authentication, and report any loss or misuse at once.
6. These duties continue after my work on Eye Drop Tracker ends.
